Do you ever wonder why we value poetry over prose? I didn't until I became an English major. I wondered about the rules of poetry, the meter, the rhythm, the rhyme. I felt the need to break free from the formal restrictions of line length and tyranny of punctuation. But then, I loved playing the game of poetry with all of its intricate rules and reasons.

Now, I'm stuck writing technical documentation in a simple editor with the spectre of artificial intelligence lurking over every typed word.

What's the message? What's the medium?

Where will my writing take me?

Will anyone read anything?

Will there be anyone to read anything?
